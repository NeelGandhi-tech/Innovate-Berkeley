The day

What to expect

Innovate@Berkeley is the largest startup expo at Berkeley and the hub for entrepreneurship. Speakers, startup booths, and challenges make for a program of events that involves the entire startup community.

Before you come

Questions

Who can attend?

Everyone!

How much does it cost?

It's free for Berkeley students! For all other attendees, the registration fee is $25.

Do I have to stay the whole time?

Feel free to come in and out as your schedule allows. However, we recommend coming by 4:30 PM to check in.

What do I do if I have more questions?

Send us an email at innovate2027@berkeley.edu.
