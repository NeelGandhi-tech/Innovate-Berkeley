Foundry An Innovate @ Berkeley initiative

Berkeley talent for venture capital firms

Foundry is a recruiting program run by Innovate @ Berkeley. We work with venture capital firms to understand who they want to hire, then introduce them to Berkeley students and recent graduates who fit the role, whether the opening is at the firm or at one of its portfolio companies.

Partner with Foundry How it works

01 Who we recruit

Candidates from across the university

Engineering and computer science

Students from EECS, the College of Engineering and data science, including those working on AI research and products.

Founders and startup operators

Students who have started their own companies or worked at early-stage startups, and who understand how young companies operate.

Business, finance and economics

Haas and economics students with experience in finance, consulting or investing, suited to investment roles at a fund or operating roles at its companies.

02 How Foundry works

From a hiring brief to an introduction

  1. 01

    Brief

    We meet with your team to learn what the role involves, which stages and sectors you invest in, and what a strong candidate looks like to you.

  2. 02

    Source

    We search the Innovate @ Berkeley and AEZ networks and the wider campus, and review each candidate against your brief.

  3. 03

    Introduce

    You get a short list with background on each candidate. We set up introductions with the people you want to meet.

03 Partnerships

How we work with firms

Searches built around your brief
Each search starts from one firm's requirements, so the candidates you see were chosen for that specific role.
Short, considered lists
We send a handful of people we think are a good fit, which keeps the time your team spends screening to a minimum.
Ongoing recruiting
Firms that hire every year can keep working with us from one recruiting season to the next. The longer we work together, the better we understand what your team looks for.

Foundry partnerships are paid. We agree on terms with each firm directly.
